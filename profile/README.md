## FixRAgent: Smarter Property Maintenance

[FixRAgent](https://fixragent.com/) helps property managers, landlords, and homeowners diagnose maintenance problems using a simple photo. Instead of relying on vague tenant complaints or unnecessary contractor visits, users can snap a picture of the issue and get a clearer understanding of the mechanical failure along with the required parts.

Built by a real estate investor and an industrial systems engineer, the platform helps reduce wasted maintenance costs and speeds up repair decisions.

### How It Works

* Take a photo of the issue
* AI analyzes the hardware problem
* Get a parts manifest for the repair
* Track repairs through a Visual Property Ledger

FixRAgent supports everything from toilet repairs and washing machine issues to commercial roof inspections.

### Why It Matters

Many maintenance costs come from misdiagnosed problems, repeat visits, and unnecessary service calls. FixRAgent helps standardize the repair process and gives property owners better visibility into hardware issues over time.

For landlords, property managers, and DIY homeowners, it means less guessing and faster fixes.
